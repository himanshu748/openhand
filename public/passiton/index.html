How this demo works · Integration status
Gemini checks meaning; the server checks exact citation excerpts. Neither replaces human review. Review is a role you can try in this demo, not a verified reviewer identity. No real scholarship, applicant records, or monetary rewards are involved.
Typed contributions and transcripts go to Google AI for checking. Recordings go to ElevenLabs only after you choose to record; the app does not retain audio. If Snowflake is connected, anonymous request metadata and approved demo answers are stored there. Do not enter personal records or private information.
