A screenshot is not a receipt.

Give to a coat drive, a medical fund, a mutual aid pot, and what comes back is a total someone typed and a photo of a spreadsheet. You cannot tell whether the money moved, when it moved, or whether it is still sitting there three weeks later.

The people running these funds are not the problem. They have no way to prove they are honest, so they ask you to assume it.

Did the money actually move?

Donations and payouts are real transfers on Solana. The balance is asked of the chain on every load and never stored here, so there is no figure for anyone to edit.

Solana

How long have they held it?

Chain events land in Snowflake, where seven accountability metrics are computed in SQL: percent disbursed, median hours from gift to payout, donations still unspent.

What does this row mean?

Gemini writes the donor update and must cite the signature behind every claim. Any sentence without a matching transaction is dropped before you read it.

Will anyone actually read it?

The receipt is spoken by ElevenLabs and paced so each number lands on its own. It is the part a donor keeps, which is what separates a trail from an update.

The model is never trusted with a number.

Gemini returns claims, each tagged with a transaction signature. Openhand discards every claim whose signature is not in the ledger, then tells you how many it threw away. A fabricated payout cannot reach the page.
