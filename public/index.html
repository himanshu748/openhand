From your voice to their answer.

  1. Speak

    Hear a question and explain it in your own words. ElevenLabs transcribes your response and speaks the follow-up.

  2. Check

    Gemini compares your explanation with the supplied handbook, flags contradictions, and asks you to correct them.

  3. Review

    Compare each claim with its source. Try the human reviewer role and approve only after checking the claims and conditions.

  4. Pass it on

    Share approved guidance through Snowflake. A sponsor can send a fixed test bounty on Solana, linked to the contribution.

A few common questions

Where does the guidance come from?

The main collection uses selected excerpts from GitHub’s public Open Source Guides, under CC BY 4.0. Source links, a pinned revision, and a checked date stay visible. English and Hindi starter answers are Pass It On adaptations. Community review remains a labelled demo role, not independent verification.

Can I still try the fictional example?

Yes. The fictional grant practice collection keeps the original correction exercise and existing evidence. It is not a real scholarship or application service.

What is stored?

Drafts and transcripts stay in your browser. Typed contributions and transcripts are sent to Gemini for source checking. Recordings go to ElevenLabs after consent; the app does not retain audio. When connected, Snowflake stores anonymous request metadata and approved demo answers. Do not enter personal records.

Do I need a wallet?

You can contribute, check, and review an answer without one. Paying the fixed 0.001 test-SOL bounty needs a Phantom wallet on devnet. This is a direct transfer, not escrow, and payment is not proof that an answer is correct.
